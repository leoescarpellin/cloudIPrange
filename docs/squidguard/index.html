How to Configure in pfSense (Step-by-Step)

  1. In the pfSense WebGUI menu, navigate to Services → SquidGuard Proxy Filter.
  2. In the General Settings tab, make sure SquidGuard is enabled (Enable).
  3. Navigate to the Blacklist tab:
    • Check the Blacklist checkbox (Enable blacklist).
    • In the Blacklist URL field, paste this mirror URL: https://leoescarpellin.github.io/cloudIPrange/squidguard/blacklists.tar.gz
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save.
  5. Switch to the Blacklist update tab and click the Download button. pfSense will download the archive, extract to /var/db/squidGuard/, and compile Berkeley DB tables (.db). Wait until the message reports Blacklist update complete.
  6. Go to the Common ACL tab (or configure group ACLs in Group ACL) and click the + icon under Target Rules List:
    • Define desired actions for each category (e.g. deny for malware, phishing, adult, cryptojacking, etc.).
  7. Click Save at the bottom of the page.
  8. Return to the General Settings tab and click Apply at the top of the screen to activate the new filtering rules in the live proxy.
Performance & Cron Tip: You can automate weekly blacklist updates in pfSense by configuring a scheduled job in the Cron package (e.g. running the SquidGuard download/update command on Sundays or Mondays) ensuring continuous protection without manual intervention.
